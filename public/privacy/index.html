Privacy

Personal Vocab Loop is built to keep your language practice private. Phrases, context tags, and voice recordings are stored in your browser’s local IndexedDB storage on this device. They are not sent to our servers.

Exports are created on your device. An encrypted export uses a passphrase you choose; we do not receive or retain that passphrase. Deleting browser site data can remove your local library, so export a backup if it matters to you.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, trackers, accounts, or third-party runtime scripts. It does not target children or collect personal data.
